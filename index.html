YAO FLAME

A youth-led design project exploring branding, product development and community impact through badminton. A Yao Design Lab project by Daniel Rong.

Sport brands · Community contribution · Social impact

The founder

Daniel Rong is a West Vancouver high school student, a member of his school badminton team, an academy program athlete, and a long-term volunteer at his local community centre.

Receiving a certificate presented by the Mayor of West Vancouver strengthened his belief that meaningful contributions, no matter how small, can make a real impact — and inspired him to do more for the community.

Through long-term training with the school team and the academy, as well as volunteer work, he realized that a high-quality, well-designed and affordable badminton racket can help more people. YAO FLAME was created to make that belief a reality.

The “flame” symbolizes growth, inner drive, and the willingness to push through challenges. Play with passion. Believe in yourself.

Brand positioning

YAO FLAME is a young, student-initiated creative brand concept built around badminton.

The finished rackets in this project focus on hands-on learning in product design and development, brand building, and social responsibility, rather than on operating as a commercial business.

Design & development

The early design stages focused on understanding racket structure, weight balance, grip comfort and aesthetic direction.

  1. Identifying key performance components: sweet spot optimization and handle ergonomics.
  2. Sketching hand-drawn concepts: frame geometry.
  3. Developing brand-aligned colour and pattern ideas: a palette inspired by flame gradients.
  4. Creating prototype visuals to show potential direction.

From there, YAO FLAME worked with a manufacturing partner to conduct research and refine the product development process.

Community contribution

Badminton racket donation

YAO FLAME is grateful to the West Vancouver Community Centre team for their longstanding service and support to local residents.

As part of its commitment to youth development and community well-being, YAO FLAME donated ten brand-new badminton rackets to the West Vancouver Community Centre, starting January 2026. The rackets were officially handed over on January 28, 2026.

Through this donation, we hope to:

  • encourage more youth and families to experience badminton
  • support active and healthy lifestyles
  • contribute to a stronger and more connected community
These rackets were designed and developed by YAO FLAME and are provided with no commercial intent, advertising or promotion. Our only purpose is to give back to the community that has supported and inspired us.

Social impact

Long-term support for students at the Siddhartha Vanasthali Institute in Nepal has shaped Daniel’s understanding of how access to education and sport can change young lives.

After participating in the UN Youth Leadership “SE4All” program, he became more aware of the responsibility that projects and brands can carry beyond the products themselves, and learned how the inclusive Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) can be integrated into real-world projects.

With this belief, YAO FLAME plans to dedicate 5% of future sales revenue to support education and sport programs for teenagers.

YAO FLAME

一个由青少年发起的设计项目,以羽毛球为载体,探索品牌、产品开发与社区影响。Yao Design Lab 旗下项目,创始人 Daniel Rong。

运动品牌 · 社区贡献 · 社会影响

创始人

Daniel Rong 是西温哥华的一名高中学生,校羽毛球队队员、青训营运动员,也是本地社区中心的长期志愿者。

从西温哥华市长手中接过那份证书,让他更加确信:再小的贡献也能带来真实的改变。这件事也促使他想为社区做得更多。

在校队和青训营的长期训练,加上志愿服务的经历,让他意识到一支高质量、设计用心且价格可负担的羽毛球拍,能够帮到更多人。YAO FLAME 就是为了把这个想法变成现实而创立的。

「火焰」象征成长、内在驱动力,以及迎难而上的意愿。以热爱出发,相信自己。

品牌定位

YAO FLAME 是一个由学生发起的、围绕羽毛球展开的创意品牌概念。

这个项目中的成品球拍,重心在于产品设计与开发、品牌建设和社会责任的动手学习,而不是作为一门商业生意来运营。

设计与开发

早期的设计阶段集中在理解球拍结构、重量平衡、握持手感与美学方向。

  1. 确定关键性能部件:甜区优化与手柄人机工程。
  2. 手绘概念草图:拍框几何。
  3. 发展贴合品牌的配色与图案:取自火焰渐变的色板。
  4. 制作原型视觉,呈现可能的方向。

在此基础上,YAO FLAME 与一家制造伙伴合作,进行研究并完善产品开发流程。

社区贡献

羽毛球拍捐赠

YAO FLAME 感谢西温哥华社区中心团队长期以来对本地居民的服务与支持。

作为对青少年发展与社区福祉承诺的一部分,YAO FLAME 自 2026 年 1 月起向西温哥华社区中心捐赠了十支全新羽毛球拍,并于 2026 年 1 月 28 日正式交接。

通过这次捐赠,我们希望:

  • 鼓励更多青少年和家庭接触羽毛球
  • 支持积极健康的生活方式
  • 为更紧密、更有联结的社区贡献一份力量
这些球拍由 YAO FLAME 设计开发,捐赠不带任何商业意图、广告或推广。我们唯一的目的,是回馈这个一直支持并激励着我们的社区。

社会影响

长期资助尼泊尔 Siddhartha Vanasthali Institute 的学生,让 Daniel 更深地理解到:教育与体育的可及性,能够改变年轻人的人生。

参加联合国青年领导力「SE4All」项目后,他更清楚地意识到项目与品牌在产品之外所能承担的责任,也学会了如何将包容性的可持续发展目标(SDGs)融入真实的项目之中。

基于这一信念,YAO FLAME 计划将未来销售收入的 5% 投入青少年教育与体育项目。
